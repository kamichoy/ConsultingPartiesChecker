Tribal Contacts

Data from HUD's Tribal Directory Assistance Tool (TDAT). TDAT was developed by the Office of Environment and Energy (OEE) to help users identify tribes that may have an interest in the location of a HUD-assisted project, and provide tribal contact information to assist users with initiating Section 106 consultation under the National Historic Preservation Act (54 U.S.C. § 300101 et seq.). Where a tribe has a Tribal Historic Preservation Officer, their contact from the NATHPO THPO Directory is shown as a cross-check.

Data sources:

  • Indiana Landmarks regional offices and staff: Indiana Landmarks, In Your Area
  • Local preservation and historical organizations: Indiana Preservation Directory and Indiana Historical Society, Find Who You Need by County
  • Tribal contacts: HUD Tribal Directory Assistance Tool (TDAT)
  • Tribal Historic Preservation Officers: NATHPO THPO Directory

Disclaimer: This is a personal project, made for convenience only. It is not an official tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the organizations above. Contact information is collected from their public websites and may be out of date or incomplete. Always confirm details with the original source before starting consultation.

Data is refreshed from these sources periodically, not on a fixed schedule.
